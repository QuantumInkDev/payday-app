Your plan.
Not our data.

Your financial data lives on your device. No FutureCheck account. No central FutureCheck server collecting your bills. Use the app offline, too.

Stay on your device.

Your bills, payments, balances, and settings are stored locally. Backups you save stay as files you control.

No analytics, tracking, or advertising in the app.

Or bring your own drive.

Optional sync uses your own Google Drive or OneDrive where offered. Financial data is end-to-end encrypted before it leaves your device. Your recovery phrase helps you unlock it on another device.

Your provider can see non-secret sync bookkeeping and encrypted file names and times, not your readable financial data. Keep your recovery phrase safe; we can't recover your vault without it if you lose every device.

Notion is a separate, optional connection. Bills, payments, and balance snapshots you choose to sync go to your Notion workspace. This data is not encrypted by FutureCheck, because Notion needs to read it.
